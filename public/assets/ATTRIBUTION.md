# Asset attribution (CC0)

The binaries in this directory are tiny vendored stand-ins generated by
`scripts/generate-assets.mjs` (run: `node scripts/generate-assets.mjs
--out public/assets`). They carry no bespoke art: each GLB is an
uncompressed box composition with flat-shaded PBR materials, and the
equirect is a 512x256 dusk-gradient JPEG. They exist so the game boots
offline, stays inside the throttled-4G load budget, and serves the
deterministic probe paths (`/assets/player-jetpack.glb`,
`/assets/danfo.glb`, `/assets/dusk-equirect.jpg`) with no CDN, CORS, or
version-drift risk.

## Upstream CC0 sources (drop-in replacements)

| Vendored file         | Upstream pack                                             | License |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| `player-jetpack.glb`  | Kenney character packs / Quaternius low-poly characters   | CC0     |
| `danfo.glb`           | Kenney vehicle packs (danfo yellow recolor)               | CC0     |
| `dusk-equirect.jpg`   | Poly Haven dusk HDRI / equirect (downsampled to 512x256)  | CC0     |

- Kenney assets: https://kenney.nl — all Kenney packs are public domain

- Quaternius packs: https://quaternius.com — public domain (CC0).
- Poly Haven HDRIs: https://polyhaven.com/hdris — public domain (CC0).

## Recolor / downsample notes

- `player-jetpack.glb`: body recolored to neon violet (`#8f7bff`) with a
  cyan emissive, jetpack to gold (`#ffd166`) — the take-light palette.
  No rigging or animation; the sim tilts the whole group.
- `danfo.glb`: van body recolored to danfo yellow (`#ffc21a`) with a
  dark window band. Traffic is rendered as a single `InstancedMesh`
  (`BoxGeometry(2, 1.2, 4.5)`), so geometry detail beyond a van
  silhouette buys nothing.
- `dusk-equirect.jpg`: downsampled to 512x256 and stored as quality-75
  JPEG for PMREM environment lighting only; fog, hemisphere, sun, and
  ambient lights carry the look if the texture fails to load.

## Swapping in full-res upstreams

1. Export/convert the upstream asset to uncompressed GLB 2.0 (`.glb`)
   or equirect JPEG/PNG.
2. Overwrite the same filename in `public/assets/` (probe paths and
   `src/game/assets.ts` URLs stay unchanged).
3. Keep each binary small enough for the offline/PWA load budget; do
   not add Draco/KTX2 decoders or WASM — `GLTFLoader` parses the
   uncompressed files with the stock three addons already in the bundle.
